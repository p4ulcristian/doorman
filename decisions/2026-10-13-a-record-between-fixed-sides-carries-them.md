# A record between fixed sides carries them

> **Built in part** (2026-10-23, v0.7.0): the sides as columns and the platform as [a kind of one scope](2026-10-23-a-member-sentence-names-a-column-or-a-kind-of-one-scope-and-what-is-held-there.md); carrying them down to the records under it waits ([Records under records wait for cellar's matching links](2026-10-23-records-under-records-wait-for-cellar.md)).

> Moved from Groundwork (2026-10-22): "Groundwork" there is doorman here where it means access. Who is rung ("the bell") became doorbell's, which tells every open screen by table.

> **Changed in part** by [Access rules are sentences in doorman's language](2026-10-17-access-rules-are-sentences-in-doormans-language.md) (2026-10-17): passes, which this is weighed against, became `:via` sentences that can also write; carrying the sides in columns stands.

> Adds to [A record of two scopes may have a parent and children](2026-10-13-a-record-of-two-scopes-may-have-a-parent-and-children.md) and Groundwork's "A row can be a pass to one record".

**Decision:** a record shared by a small, fixed set of sides that all read and write it (a conversation between a customer, a shop, a partner and the platform's staff) carries those sides in its own columns: a scope column per scope side, a person column for the person. It is an ordinary record of several scopes; the records under it carry the same sides.

For that, **a person column is carried down like a scope column:** a record under a record with a person on it has the person too, filled from the parent and tied to it by the matching link.

A side that is "the platform's staff" is the platform's own scope, not an empty column.

**Why:** every side writes, and a pass only reads. The sides are known and few, so they fit in columns, where the wall compares them directly and the bell names exactly them. A table of participants next to the record would be passes that write, on a record nobody owns: two new abilities for a case columns already cover.

**The price:** the number of sides is fixed by the table. A record with any number of members (a group chat) doesn't fit and waits for a shape of its own.

**Instead of:** a record with no owner that is seen only through passes, with passes that write.
